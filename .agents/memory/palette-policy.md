---
name: Palette policy
description: Approved semantic palette policy and intentionally deferred styling scope.
---
Use the approved “בטון ותוכנית” palette. Existing Tailwind family names retain their semantic meanings; named tokens are intended for new code, not a wholesale rename of existing classes. Green and emerald intentionally share one ramp. Brand orange is decorative, not a text background.
**Why:** The user approved a centralized palette change instead of thousands of component edits.
**How to apply:** Keep later styling changes consistent with these meanings. Inline legacy colors, category-color families, fonts, emoji, small type, dark mode and bottom navigation require separately scoped work; do not silently expand palette batches to clean them up.
