---
name: Palette policy
description: Approved semantic palette policy and intentionally deferred styling scope.
---
Existing Tailwind family names retain their semantic meanings; named tokens are intended for new code, not a wholesale rename of existing classes. Green and emerald intentionally share one ramp. Brand orange remains the action color, not decorative-only. The user's latest “clean & vivid” direction replaces muted status families and warm neutrals with full-strength status colors, cool slate, softer dark headers and golden warnings distinct from action orange.
**Why:** The user found the muted palette “faded, grey, dark” on the phone. They explicitly accept the supplied below-4.5:1 white-on-green-600, white-on-amber-500 and white-on-orange-500 pairings for this direction. Do not claim universal AA compliance or independently darken these colors; structural sunlight-readability improvements remain separate work.
**How to apply:** Keep later styling changes consistent with these meanings. Inline legacy colors, category-color families, fonts, emoji, small type, dark mode and bottom navigation require separately scoped work; do not silently expand palette batches to clean them up.
